# Oniko Fit — content for Bill to review

Everything in `plugin/oniko-fit/data/program.json`. Coach cues from Bill are used nearly verbatim. These parts were written by Donald and need Bill's eye:

1. **Band Pull-Apart (optional, Home A):** 2 x 12–15. Setup, steps, feel (upper back, back of shoulders), mistakes (shrugging, bent elbows, arching, letting it snap back). Weight field: log 0 or a band number.
2. **DB Glute Bridge (optional, Home A):** 2 x 10–15. Setup (DB across hips, towel pad), steps (drive through heels, 1 s squeeze), mistakes (arching at top, toes, knees in, rushing). Start: body weight, then 10–15 lb.
3. **Band Squat, Band Row, Band Press cues** (listed as options on Goblet Squat, DB Row and DB Overhead Press cards).
4. **Chair box squat** option text (from Bill's note, my wording).
5. **Hammer Iso-Lateral Shoulder Press setup** ("seat so handles start about chin or ear level, back on the pad") — Bill's cue was shared with the DB press.
6. **Setup/steps split** for every card: I split Bill's one-paragraph cues into Setup + numbered steps and added small connective steps (e.g. "Unlock the machine and stand tall", "Grip the handles").
7. **One-arm row logging:** "do all reps on one side, then switch; log the reps for one side."
8. **Push-up surface ladder** labels: Counter, Stairs or low table, Knees on the floor, Full push-up.
9. **Engine choices:** Easy + one rep short of 12 on all sets counts as ready; Easy adds +2 reps to the target; deload = ~12% lighter and 2 sets; "low energy for 2 weeks" = every session in the last 14 days (min 3) flagged; comeback 90% after 8+ days, 80% after 14+.
10. **Warm-up card:** marching 1 min, arm circles 1 min, 10–15 slow bodyweight squats, then a light set of the first exercise logged as Warm-up.

## Bill's review (2026-11-04) — applied in 1.0.1

Approved as-is apart from these changes, all applied:

1. Hammer bench and shoulder press go up 2.5 lb per side, with the note "If your gym has 2.5 lb plates, use them for presses. If not, add 5 lb per side instead." V-squat and iso row stay at 5 lb per side.
2. One-arm row: weaker side first, match reps and weight on the other side, log the weaker side.
3. Chair box squat: chair against a wall, touch lightly, don't plop down.
4. Band row, press, squat and pull-apart: band check, anchor and snap-path safety line. Band row uses a door anchor at about chest height.
5. Gym DB deadlift: no jump bigger than about 20%. When the next dumbbell is too far (for example 20 to 25), she holds the weight and adds a set of 2, then jumps. An extra set doesn't count as a missed 5x5.

Also: "ribs down" added to the glute bridge. The push-up level now moves only after 12 clean reps on every set, never on "Easy" alone. Engine checks (tests): Easy at 8 adds reps, not load, and the rep target is capped at 12.
